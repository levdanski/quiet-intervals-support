QUIET INTERVALS

Your practice is personal.

Privacy policy prepared for the 1.1 update. Last updated 4 October 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

Quiet Intervals stores preset names, durations, cue times, sound choices, appearance preferences and session checkpoints locally. The timer and six included sounds work offline.

Optional meditation history

History recording is off by default. If you enable it, the app saves dates, actual unpaused practice time, active time segments, completion status and a snapshot of each session’s preset name and planned duration. It records only sessions run through Quiet Intervals. Preparation, pauses and the final sound’s decay are excluded. Interrupted records contain only the last saved measured time and are marked incomplete.

Enabling recording during a session applies to the next session. Disabling stops the unfinished record and future recording; earlier saved records remain. In History, open a session record to delete that individual record. Settings lets you confirm deletion of all history. Deleting all history during an active session prevents that session from restoring a record. If a storage change cannot be saved, the app shows an error instead of claiming deletion succeeded.

No developer data collection

The app does not send presets or history to the developer. There is no app account, server, cloud synchronisation, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not access HealthKit, record audio or use the microphone.

Export and backups

CSV export happens only when you choose Export CSV. The app prepares a local file and presents the iPhone sharing controls; the destination you select controls its copy. Exported files can contain session names, dates and practice time. Deleting a local record does not delete previously exported copies.

Normal iPhone backups managed by Apple may include presets and history according to your device settings. Local deletion does not erase existing backups. Manage backups through Apple’s settings. Deleting the app removes its current local data; backups remain separate.

Offline learning and optional links

Learning articles and their research summaries are bundled with the app. Opening a reference, SamadhiHub, support or privacy link makes a normal request through your browser. Quiet Intervals does not attach session records or presets to these links. Destination websites have their own privacy policies. Opening them is optional.

This support website

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and processes website requests under its privacy statement. The app does not contact the website automatically.
