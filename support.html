QUIET INTERVALS

Make time your own.

Support information for version 1.1.1, 23 October 2026.

Create a routine once

On Meditate, create a preset, name it and set its duration. Hours, minutes and seconds wheels let you set precise times. More hours expands the range in blocks of 24; existing longer values remain editable. Add elapsed sound cues, choose sounds and save. Up to 60 cues are supported. If shortening a session conflicts with a cue, correct that cue before saving.

Evenly spaced reminders

In the preset editor, choose Divide into periods, then select 1–59 periods. The app divides the full session duration equally and creates opening and closing sounds with reminders at the boundaries between periods. Choose the opening/closing level, then either 50% of that level or a Custom level for reminders. One period has no intermediate reminders. Apply equal periods replaces the cues in the draft; Save preset keeps the changes. Existing saved volumes remain unchanged unless you apply new settings. Your iPhone media volume still controls the overall output.

Start, pause and return

Select a saved preset and Begin. Audio preparation is followed by a three-second lead-in. Each cue plays once, followed by silence. You can pause, resume or end. The final cue marks the planned duration and plays once. If you continue meditating, total time and extra time remain visible until you tap End session. History saves actual unpaused practice, including that extra time. Editing a preset does not change a running session.

Volume and offline use

The native volume control changes media volume on the current speaker or headphones. All sounds are local and use no notification permission. Playback is designed for locked-screen use, Do Not Disturb and Airplane Mode. Preview at a comfortable level. Calls, audio interruptions and output disconnects may pause playback; check the route before resuming. Force-quitting the app ends a running transport; missed cues are not replayed automatically. A saved paused session can be reopened and resumed.

Choose whether to record history

History is off by default. Enable it in History or Settings before your next session. Timer records contain measured, unpaused practice in Quiet Intervals. The outcome is Completed, Ended early or Interrupted. Interrupted time is a lower bound through the last saved checkpoint. Enabling during a session starts recording with the next session; disabling stops the unfinished record. Earlier saved records remain.

Add meditation completed without the timer

With history enabled, open History and choose Add session. Enter a session name, its start date and time, and a whole-number duration from 1 to 1,440 minutes. The session must already have finished. Save session adds a local, self-reported record to your totals; it does not change your presets or current timer. Manual entries are labelled Manually entered, so they remain distinct from practice the app timed.

Read your figures

Day, Week, Month and All time use your current device timezone. Weeks run Monday–Sunday. Practice crossing midnight is split between days; a session is counted once on its original start date. Average length uses full recorded durations of sessions started in the selected period. Sessions per day divides that count by elapsed calendar days. Figures describe recorded practice only; Not recorded and recording gaps do not imply you did not meditate.

All-time totals include every saved record. For readability, its chart shows at most the most recent 30 calendar days, labelled as a subset. Switch to Figures for the complete daily numerical alternative. Incomplete records are identified in totals and averages.

Delete or export

In History, open a session record to delete it, or confirm Delete all history in Settings. Deleting all during a session prevents that session from restoring a record. Export CSV opens the iPhone share sheet for a CSV file. Its Source column identifies Timer and Manually entered records; manual durations are self-reported. Exports and device backups are managed separately from local deletion. Storage errors are reported without stopping meditation cues.

Learn and appearance

Seven offline guides offer practical introductions. Christ’s Letters is first and includes the complete, source-attributed Meditation Prayer recommended for memorisation, with a spiritual practice summary. Its teachings are identified as spiritual practice, without a claimed clinical benefit or timetable of attainment. The other guides distinguish traditional context from research findings. Study windows describe specific interventions and populations; they do not forecast your progress. Use a preset opens existing preset selection without changing an active session. In Settings, choose System, Light or Dark; System follows your iPhone appearance.
