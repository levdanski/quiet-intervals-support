QUIET INTERVALS

Make time your own.

Quiet Intervals is currently in development. Public App Store availability has not yet been announced.

Create a routine once

Choose Create a preset, name your routine, set its duration and add sound cues at elapsed times. Choose a default sound or give individual cues their own sounds. Save, then select this preset whenever you return.

Start your session

Select a preset and tap Begin. Allow notifications and sounds when prompted. The app takes a short preparation period to schedule cues before time zero. You can pause, resume or end your session.

If you cannot hear a cue

In iPhone Settings, allow notifications and Sounds for Quiet Intervals. Turn Silent mode off, check alert volume, and allow the app in any active Focus. Cues obey iPhone settings. Test a short session on your chosen speaker or headphones before meditating.

How many cues can I use?

Each preset supports up to 60 cues, including start and finish sounds if you add them. There is no app-imposed limit on saved preset count. Choose any positive duration in whole seconds that your device can schedule.

Does editing a preset change my current session?

No. Your current session keeps its own snapshot. The saved changes apply next time you begin that preset.
